# Privacy Policy for Gezegeni Koru (Defend the Planet)

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application **Gezegeni Koru** (Bundle ID: `com.neonvoid.cyberdefense`), developed by İskender Bedirhan Yılmaz.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Gezegeni Koru does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number). All gameplay progress, high scores, and audio settings are stored locally on your device.

## 2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)
The App uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads:
- **Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA):** On iOS 14.5 and later, we request permission via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before accessing device advertising identifiers. If denied, only non-personalized ads are served.
- **Diagnostics & Usage Data:** Google AdMob may collect crash logs, performance metrics, and device identifiers to optimize ad delivery and detect fraudulent activity.
- For more details, review Google's Privacy Policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit)
In-app purchases (such as the "VIP Reklamsız" lifetime unlock) are processed directly and securely by Apple via StoreKit. We do not receive, process, or store your credit card or financial account information.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
